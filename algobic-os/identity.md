# ALGOBIC: Company Identity

## 1. WHAT ALGOBIC IS 🟢

**A proving ground.**

The place where ambitious students find out whether they are actually good, judged by people who would know.

Not a school. Not a course. Not a placement service. An institution that holds a bar.

---

## 2. CATEGORY 🟢 (internal) · 🔴 (public language)

**Internal:** standards body for young builders.

**Public-facing name: 🔴 PLACEHOLDER.** Candidates: "proving ground", "the bar", "examining body for builders".
Blocked on E3: the words must come from students, not from us. Build everything around the concept; leave the noun open.

## 3. VISION: 10 years 🟢

> In ten years, "I cleared ALGOBIC" is the fastest way an Indian student proves they are good.

Testable. Decade-scale. Doesn't name a mechanism, so the product can evolve without breaking the vision.

---

## 4. MISSION: now 🟢

> Hold a bar worth clearing, and publish who clears it.

---

## 5. CORE PROMISE 🟢

> You will find out the truth about how good you are. And if you are good, everyone will know.

Two parts, both scarce: **truth** (nobody gives students an honest measurement) and **standing** (nobody can prove they're good).

---

## 6. POSITIONING STATEMENT 🟡

For ambitious engineering students in India's tier-2 and tier-3 cities who suspect they are capable of more than their placement suggests, **ALGOBIC is a proving ground (not a course)** that tells them the truth about where they stand, and makes it public when they clear the bar.

Unlike bootcamps that sell instruction and guarantee outcomes, ALGOBIC sells nothing but the standard. Most people do not clear it on the first attempt.

*Assumption: students will tolerate being judged. Blocked on E3. If false, this identity is wrong and the work-marketplace thesis returns.*

---

## 7. DIFFERENTIATION 🟢

| We | Everyone else |
|---|---|
| Hold a bar | Deliver a syllabus |
| Publish the failure rate | Publish the success stories |
| Borrow authority from named judges | Assert authority from marketing |
| Sell the attempt | Sell the course |
| Get harder to copy every year | Get easier to copy every year |
| Say "not yet" | Say "congratulations" |

**The structural moat: authority is time-denominated.** A bar's meaning accrues with every year it is held and every person it turns away. Time cannot be bought, copied, or funded around.

---

## 8. STRATEGIC REFUSALS 🟢

Public. Specific. Non-negotiable. A category is defined by what it refuses.

1. **Never sell instruction.** AI teaches better, free, forever.
2. **Never guarantee a job.** Guarantees are either lies or refund liabilities.
3. **Never pass everyone.** A bar everyone clears is not a bar. The failure rate is published.
4. **Never sell a better verdict.** Money buys attempts, never outcomes.
5. **Never make the founder the authority.** The panel holds the bar. *(Buildspace shut down in Aug 2024 with two years of runway and its best season on record. Cause: founder burnout. This refusal is a structural safeguard, not modesty.)*
6. **Never serve two audiences at once.** Students first. Employers later. *(On Deck's documented cause of death was breadth.)*
7. **Never use fear of unemployment as the hook.** It is factually false: CSE employability in India is above 80%.
8. **Never make a claim we cannot link to a person, a date, or a number.**

>

**Why this replaces the old version:** the old manifesto opened on "the job market changed / education didn't" and implied unemployment. That claim is false at 80%+ CSE employability, and a false premise is a weak foundation for a standards brand. The new version concedes the job, then attacks its *quality* (a ceiling problem, not a floor problem). Fear of mediocrity, not fear of unemployment.

**"Build Before They Do" survives unchanged.** It works better for a proving ground than it did for a school.

## 10. BRAND PRINCIPLES 🟢

Brand-specific. The 10 Enduring Principles remain the company-level layer.

1. **Speak like a judge, not a coach.** Unimpressed by effort. Impressed by work.
2. **Restraint is authority.** Institutions do not shout.
3. **The record is the hero.** Never the founder, never the curriculum, never us.
4. **Publish what students did, never what we taught.**
5. **Show the bar being enforced.** Rejections are more credible than successes.
6. **Cheap to attempt, standing earned.** Price for access; never sell status.
7. **Local before global.** Trust localizes as content globalizes.
8. **Every claim carries a name, a date, or a number.**

## 12. BRAND VOICE 🟢

**Calm. Specific. Unhurried. Says "not yet" without apologizing.**

| ❌ Never | ✅ Instead |
|---|---|
| "Unlock your potential" | "Find out where you stand" |
| "Transform your career" | "Clear the bar" |
| "Join thousands of learners" | "41 attempted. 9 cleared." |
| "Guaranteed placement" | "We do not place anyone" |
| "Start your journey today" | "Attempt it" |
| "Industry-leading curriculum" | "There is no curriculum" |
| "You've got this!" | "Most people don't, first time" |

**Banned words:** unlock · empower · revolutionize · journey · next-generation · game-changing · cutting-edge · seamless · transform · 10x · hustle · grind · guaranteed · potential · dream

**Rules:**
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns.
- No adjective doing a verb's job.
- Never motivational. Never fear-mongering. Never cute.
- Numbers over adverbs. "9 of 41" beats "a select few."
- Second person, present tense.
- One idea per sentence. One claim per paragraph.
