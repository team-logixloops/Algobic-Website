## THE ONE PARAGRAPH

ALGOBIC is where you go to build the thing you scrolled past. Every day people see an AI project on Instagram or LinkedIn, feel the itch, and never build it, not because it's hard, but because nobody shows the path and nobody is waiting for it. We close that gap. No degree, no coding background, no prerequisite: you arrive with an idea you saw, you leave with a thing that exists. We are not a school and we sell no courses. We are the front door to building, for the generation that finally has the tools and has never been shown the door.

## THE ESSAY

### What we do

We convert scrolling into shipping.

Somebody watches a 30-second clip of an AI app and thinks *I wish I could make that.* In 2026 they can (the tools got good enough), and they still don't. Not from lack of capability. From lack of a path, a deadline, and anyone noticing.

ALGOBIC gives all three. You pick a thing you saw. We show you the exact route from nothing to shipped. There's a deadline, and there are other people doing it at the same time, and at the end the thing is real and public with your name on it.

We do not teach. There is no syllabus, no module list, no certificate. AI already teaches better than any instructor, free, at 3am. Selling instruction in 2026 is selling ice in winter.

### Why now, and not two years ago

Because the requirement disappeared.

*(Figures below verified at source 2026-07-30. Weak ones are labelled. Do not repeat them in marketing without the label.)*

The AI app-builder market is estimated at **$4.7B in 2026**, forecast $12.3B next year at ~38% CAGR *(the source flags $4.7B as an estimate)*. **41-46% of new code written globally is AI-generated.**

**Lovable: ~$400M ARR by Feb/Mar 2026**, up from $100M in mid-2024, valued at $6.6B in Dec 2025. **Replit: $150M ARR by Sept 2025**, valued at $9B in Mar 2026, publicly targeting $1B ARR by end-2026. **Bolt: $40M ARR within ~6 months of launch.** **Base44: $100M ARR by early 2026**, acquired by Wix ~6 months after launch. India has its own: **Emergent AI, $25M ARR run rate within months, $23M Series A led by Lightspeed with Prosus and YC.**

On the non-technical share: reported figures range **63% to 84%** of AI app-builder users having no coding background, and **neither number publishes a methodology.** ⚠️ Directionally consistent, individually unreliable. The better-sourced framing: roughly **100-120 million people build business applications on no-code platforms against 27.7 million professional developers**, a ~4:1 ratio. Gartner-attributed: **70% of new enterprise applications will use low-code or no-code by 2026**, up from under 25% in 2020, and **80% of low-code users will sit outside IT departments**, up from 60% in 2021.

The tools solved *capability*. Nobody solved *activation*.

That's the whole opportunity. Every one of those companies spends its money making the tool better and almost nothing making people actually start. There are 750+ no-code platforms and a near-total absence of anyone standing at the entrance saying "here, this one, start with this, today."

Two years ago this business couldn't exist: you'd have had to teach people to code first. Two years from now the door will have a crowd at it. The window is now.

### Who it's for

Anyone who has looked at something built and thought *I could never*.

Deliberately not engineers-only. A commerce student, a designer, a shop owner, a second-year CSE kid: same promise, same day, no prerequisite. Dropping the engineering requirement is what makes this large rather than niche, and it's honest, because the requirement genuinely no longer exists.

Primary: 18-25, India, tier-2 and tier-3 cities. Not because they're desperate, but because they have the most upside and the least access to anyone who has actually shipped something.

⚠️ **Important correction on the employability claim.** India Skills Report 2026 (ETS with CII, AICTE, AIU, Taggd; 1 lakh+ candidates via the Global Employability Test, 1,000+ organisations) puts **CS engineers at 80% and IT engineers at 78% employability.** But "employability" there is measured by a *test score*, not by actual employment outcomes. So it weakens the "you won't get a job" framing without proving everyone gets one. Use it to avoid a false claim, not to make a confident opposite claim.

### Our unfair advantage

**Taste.**

When production becomes free, output stops being scarce and judgment becomes scarce. The internet is about to drown in AI slop. The rare thing is the person who knows what's worth making and whether the result is any good.

The founder has it and it's already public: a hand-written canvas particle system, a hand-bumped sitemap `lastmod` with a comment explaining crawler churn, four dependencies where most people ship forty, structured data emitted as separate blocks because some validators skip graph-wrapped nodes. That is not prompted. That's judgment, and it's visible at algobic.in right now.

Which makes the pitch self-proving. We don't claim anyone can build good things with these tools. We demonstrate it, publicly, and the demonstration *is* the marketing.

**The honest limit:** taste is an execution advantage, not a distribution one. Distribution is the constraint in this market and we currently have almost none. Everything in `content.md` exists to fix that.

### What we are not

- **Not a school.** No courses, no modules, no certificates. If AI does it free, we don't sell it.
- **Not a tool.** Lovable, Bolt, Replit and Emergent are funded and enormous. We are the layer above them: which one, for what, starting how.
- **Not a placement service.** We never guarantee a job. Guarantees are lies or refund liabilities.
- **Not for engineers only.** The moment we sound like a dev brand, we lose the larger half of the market.
- **Not motivational.** No hustle, no grind, no "you've got this." Demonstration, not encouragement.

### What compounds

Content churns. Two things accumulate:

1. **A public record of what people actually built here**: one page per person, one page per thing shipped. That's the asset a competitor can't clone by copying our content.
2. **Taste as a recognisable signature.** Once "it looks like an Algobic build" means something, it can't be bought.

### Voice

A builder showing you their screen. Not a teacher, not a coach.

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Numbers over adverbs. Never motivational, never fear-mongering, never cute. Every claim carries a name, a date, or a number.

**Banned:** unlock · empower · revolutionize · journey · next-generation · game-changing · cutting-edge · seamless · transform · 10x · hustle · grind · guaranteed · potential · dream · unleash

| ❌ Never | ✅ Instead |
|---|---|
| "Unlock your potential" | "Here's the thing you'll have by tonight" |
| "Join thousands of learners" | "37 people built this last month" |
| "Master AI development" | "Build one thing. Then another." |
| "Start your journey" | "Start with this" |
| "Industry-leading curriculum" | "There's no curriculum" |
| "You've got this!" | "Most people stall at step 3. Here's step 3." |

### Design philosophy

Restraint reads as confidence. Ornament reads as insecurity.

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| Type | Orbitron 700 display / Inter text. Already in place. Keep. |
| Colour | One accent, earned. Light and dark both first-class. Already implemented. |
| Space | Generous. Emptiness is a claim about confidence. |
| Motion | Clarifies causality only. The existing view-transition theme toggle is the standard. |
| Hierarchy | One decision per screen. |
| The work | Shipped things are the hero. Never the founder, never us. |
| Craft | Users infer competence from interface quality. This is the live advantage. Protect it. |

### Tagline

**Build Before They Do.** Unchanged. Says nothing about code, which is exactly right now.
